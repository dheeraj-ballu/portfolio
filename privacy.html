Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data securely

Last Updated: May 16, 2026
Quick Navigation

📝

Information Collected

At Gurpreet Kaur Academy, we only collect the information necessary to provide you with the best educational experience. The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical address provided during booking.
  • Academic Information: Current grade level, subjects of interest, prior academic records (if shared by you), and learning goals.
  • Payment Information: Billing details required to process transactions securely (note: we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers).
⚙️

How User Data is Used

We utilize the collected information strictly for educational and administrative purposes:

  • To schedule, manage, and deliver personalized online or offline tutoring sessions.
  • To communicate with students and parents regarding progress, class schedules, and updates.
  • To process payments securely and manage billing subscriptions.
  • To improve our curriculum and website performance based on user feedback.
🍪

Cookies & Analytics

Our website utilizes "cookies" and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies help us understand how you interact with our site, enabling us to load pages faster and remember your preferences (such as Light/Dark mode settings).

We may use anonymous analytics tools to track website traffic, but this data does not personally identify you. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer.

🤝

Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. However, we may share essential data with trusted third-party service providers solely to facilitate our services:

  • Video Conferencing: Platforms like Zoom or Google Meet to conduct online classes.
  • Payment Gateways: Providers like Stripe or Razorpay to process payments securely.

These third parties are bound by their own strict privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.

🔒

Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted administrative access, to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

🛡️

User Rights

You have full control over your personal data. At any time, you hold the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data from our systems (subject to legal or administrative requirements).
  • Opt-out of any promotional emails or newsletters.
👶

Children’s Privacy

Protecting the privacy of young students is especially important. Our services are directed toward parents or legal guardians when enrolling students under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without verifiable parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information.
