❓ Quick Answers

Common Questions

How quickly will I get a response?
▼

We typically respond within 24 hours via email.

Can I get a demo class first, and what is the cost?
▼

Absolutely! We offer a 45-minute demo/trial class for all new students for just ₹99. If you aren't satisfied, we offer a 50% refund. If you continue with any selected course plan, the ₹99 will be fully adjusted/deducted from your plan. Simply fill out the form above to schedule yours!

Do you offer group discounts?
▼

Yes! We offer special discounted rates for groups of 3+ students enrolling together. Contact us to discuss custom group plans and pricing.

What platforms are used for online classes?
▼

Online classes are conducted primarily via Zoom or Google Meet with an interactive digital whiteboard. All sessions are recorded and shared with students within 24 hours.
