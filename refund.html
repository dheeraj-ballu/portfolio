Refund Policy

Fair and transparent guidelines on cancellations and refunds

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

✅

Eligibility for Refunds

At Gurpreet Kaur Academy, we strive for 100% student satisfaction. We offer refunds under the following circumstances:

  • If a planned batch or class is permanently canceled by the educator before the cycle begins.
  • If the student faces persistent, irresolvable technical issues caused directly by our platform infrastructure during online classes.
  • If a refund is requested within the initial "cooling-off" period (the first 2 classes of a new monthly subscription).
🚫

Non-Refundable Services

Please note that the following fees and services are strictly non-refundable:

  • One-time registration or admission fees.
  • Digital products, study materials, or PDF notes once downloaded or sent via email.
  • Classes missed by the student without prior notice or valid justification.
  • Subscriptions canceled in the middle of a billing cycle (after the cooling-off period).
⏰

Cancellation Window

If you wish to cancel your monthly subscription and receive a partial refund, you must notify us within the first 48 hours after attending your second class. Any cancellation requests made after this window will be applicable for the next billing cycle, and no current-month refund will be issued.

🔄

Rescheduling Policy

We understand that emergencies happen. If a student is unable to attend a scheduled 1-on-1 session, they may request a reschedule.

  • Reschedule requests must be made at least 12 hours prior to the class start time.
  • A maximum of 2 rescheduled classes are allowed per month.
  • Failure to provide notice will result in the class being marked as "Attended" and deducted from the monthly quota without a refund.
⏳

Processing Time for Refunds

Once a refund request is approved, the amount will be processed and credited back to the original payment method. Please allow 5 to 7 business days for the funds to appear in your account, depending on your bank or payment provider's processing times.

🌟

Exceptional Circumstances

We approach our educational services with empathy. In the event of severe medical emergencies, family loss, or natural disasters, refund or pause-subscription policies may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis at the sole discretion of the academy management.

📞

Contact Support

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, or if you need to reschedule a class, please contact our support team with your student details and the reason for your request.
